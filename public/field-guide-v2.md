# One ordinary situation. An honest inquiry.

Sovereign Psychology Observatory · Educational field guide v2.0 · 25 October 2026

Original framework: Samuel Raymond Clow. Educational example and editorial synthesis: AI-assisted.

1. **Describe the event.** A colleague says your proposal overlooks a recurring cost. The comment is observed; the motive and size of an error remain unknown.
2. **Separate two appraisals.** Describe perceived demand and perceived coping resources independently. Neither is a diagnosis or a measured nervous-system state.
3. **Compare responses.** Curiosity asks for the calculation; competence checks it. A boundary addresses conduct if needed. A pause makes room to review. Unsupported motive stories do not answer facts.
4. **Choose conduct.** Ask for the cost breakdown. State a prediction and what would change the plan.
5. **Introduce evidence.** If an omitted cost is documented, revise the proposal. Feeling calm is not required to correct the work.
6. **Distinguish test types.** A written or imagined reply is rehearsal. A role-play is a different test. A real conversation supplies evidence in that context. One attempt does not establish durable general competence.
7. **Revise and retain.** Record observations, limits, failed or inconclusive outcomes, and a next test. Save only by choice and keep a portable export.

This is education for manageable ordinary situations. It is not a clinical treatment, trauma protocol, physiological measurement or reconsolidation procedure. No intensity target, repetition prescription or cure guarantee is given. The integrated Sovereignty Method remains a working framework whose additional benefit is untested.
